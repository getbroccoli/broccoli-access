# Thesaurus

The words Broccoli Access's code and docs use, and what each one means. Only Broccoli Access's own terms belong here; well-known ones such as job, model or mapping do not.

- **Instance**: one installation of Broccoli Access with its own database, for one company. The `instance` table holds its single row.
- **Owner**: the user who set up the instance. Identified by the user id, never by the email.
- **User**: a person who can sign in (Better Auth's `user` table).
- **Permission profile**: a named set of what a user may do. Everyone has `EMPLOYEE`, and the owner has `OWNER`, which holds the grants of `ADMIN`. Not "role", which sounds like a job, nor "access level".
- **Built-in**: defined in Broccoli Access's code and changed by its releases, such as the built-in permission profiles. Not "system", which means Broccoli Access acting on its own.
- **Account**: one way a user signs in, such as their Google account (`google`) (Better Auth's `account` table).
- **Session**: a signed-in browser, carried in a cookie and stored in the `session` table.
- **Setup**: the first-run flow of a new instance: the setup holder connects Google Workspace with the Broccoli app or their own Google app, and the Google super admin who consents becomes the owner, signed in; then the workspace is scanned and the owner selects the company's apps.
- **Setup step**: where a new instance is in setup: `connect_google`, `scan`, `select_apps`, then `done`.
- **Redirect address**: `<public origin>/api/google/callback`, where Google sends the admin back after consent. The admin registers it for their Google app; every Google consent and sign-in comes back through it.
- **Setup session**: before the owner exists, the server-side record that one browser holds setup: the hash of a token in the `broccoli_access_setup` cookie, stored by the first start that carries a token, and valid three days after the holder last opened setup. Setup stays locked to it, even after it expires, until the database is dropped.
- **Setup holder**: the browser holding the setup session, and the security context it acts as. It may connect Google Workspace, which creates the owner, and nothing else.
- **Data folder**: `DATA_DIR`, the folder for files that must survive restarts, such as `secrets/`.
- **File**: bytes with a MIME type that a module stores in the `files` table, such as an employee's avatar. The module that references it decides who may see it.
- **Record**: a module's main entity, such as an employee.
- **Subject**: the employee a record is about, which permission scopes are measured against.
- **History**: every change to a record, kept as history lines in the `record_history` table and shown on the record. The product word; "audit" stays internal and means a separate security trail.
- **History line**: one change to one record: its change author, when, its change cause, its change reason and each changed field's old and new value. Appended in the transaction of the change, and never deleted.
- **Change author**: who made a change to a record, from the security context: a signed-in user, Broccoli Access itself or the setup holder.
- **Change cause**: why a change to a record happened or what started it, such as `EDITED_DIRECTLY`, with the id of what caused it when there is one. The module whose action is the cause declares it.
- **Change reason**: why, in the change author's own words: `changeReason` on an input, the reason on its history line.
- **Employee**: someone who works for the company. The People area lists employees.
- **Suspended with access**: an employee suspended in the directory who still has an active account in another approved app. Computed when read; the People tab and the sidebar badge count them.
- **Security context**: who is calling application code: a signed-in user, and whether they are the owner; the setup holder; or the system.
- **System**: Broccoli Access acting on its own, such as creating the owner at setup.
- **Domain event**: something that happened in the domain, published by one module in a transaction and handled by others after the commit, such as `records.changed`.
- **Connectors container**: the container image, built from a separate repository, that holds the connectors for every app except Google Workspace. The core calls it through the connector contract. It keeps no state: each call carries the connection, and the core saves what comes back.
- **Robot**: a connector that works an app's admin pages in a browser, for an app without an API on the company's plan.
- **Runtime**: where a connector's call runs: `server`, the connectors container by itself, or `owner_browser`, the owner's Chrome through the extension. Not the Node.js runtime.
- **App session**: a signed-in session in an app, the integration user's or the owner's own, used by a connector. It is handed over by the extension or made by server sign-in. Not a session, which is a browser signed in to Broccoli Access.
- **Extension**: the Broccoli Access extension for Chrome. It hands over app sessions.
- **Disable, Enable**: the UI words for suspending an app account and making it active again (`suspend` and `reactivate` in code). A suspended account shows as "Disabled".
- **Invite**: the UI word for asking a connected app to send its own invitation to an employee who has no account in it (`invite` in code).
- **Invited**: an employee with no account in a connected app whose latest invite there succeeded or was done by hand, until a scan finds their account; the row shows "Invited on <date>".
- **Account change**: one Invite, Disable, Enable or Remove, from the owner's click to its result (`account_changes`).
- **Done by hand**: a failed account change that the owner made in the app themselves, then confirmed with "I did it by hand".
- **Activity**: the page listing every account change, newest first. Not History, which belongs to one record.
- **Integration user**: the Google Workspace user that Broccoli Access creates for itself, which the owner makes an admin in each app and connectors act as. Not "robot", which is a connector, nor "service account", which is an app account.
- **Connector method**: how a connector reaches its app: the official API, the internal API, or a robot. The connector picks; the owner does not.
- **Internal API**: the calls an app's own pages make, used with an app session.
- **Handed over**: the owner's own app session, which they signed in to in Chrome and the extension sent to the instance.
- **Server sign-in**: Broccoli Access signing in to an app as the integration user by itself, in a browser on the server, with "Sign in with Google" or the code the app sends to its mailbox.
- **Remove**: the UI word for deleting an app account, to free its seat (`remove` in code). It cannot be undone. Not "delete".
- **Account without a person**: an app account linked to no employee, and not marked as a service or external account.
- **Service account**: an app account that software uses, such as a bot or the integration user.
- **External account**: an app account of someone outside the company, such as a contractor. It has a note and no person page.
- **Template**: a named list of apps, each with a role, that the owner assigns to people. Not "policy", which sounds like a rule that runs by itself.
- **Provision**: invite a person to every app they are missing, in one step.
- **Difference**: where a person's accounts do not match their templates: **missing**, an app they should have, or **extra**, an account no template of theirs gives. Broccoli Access lists differences and never fixes one by itself.
- **Offboard**: disable every account of a person in one step and take their templates off.
- **Connector factory**: a private repository where agents investigate an app, explore a test workspace of it, and check its connector against the real app every week.
- **Parked**: an app in the connector factory that waits for a person, for a payment, a CAPTCHA or a phone check.
- **Mail choice**: how the instance sends email: the owner's SMTP server, their Resend key, or no email. Saved on the instance in Settings.

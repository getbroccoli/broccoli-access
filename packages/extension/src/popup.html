Broccoli Access

The extension pairs Chrome with your Broccoli Access instance. When you connect or reconnect an app through the extension, it asks permission to send cookies from the domains shown in the Hand over window and their subdomains, plus any requested browser storage values, such as a sign-in token. If you choose Hand over, Broccoli Access can use that session to manage user accounts in the app.
